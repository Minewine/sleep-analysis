# 11. Consolidation map

**Sources:** the eleven area sections of this draft — their consolidation candidates (N.7; section 0 contributes the doc-drift table and consolidation notes of its 0.7 documentation map) and their open questions and defects (N.8; section 0: 0.9) — plus the mechanical evidence kotlin-reference-scan.md and lint-summary.md under evidence/ (scan tables A–C, lint UnusedResources). Every row below was already verified twice: written by the area author, then re-checked against the code by an independent reviewer. This map only merges rows across areas — it adds no new findings. Sections are cited by number (0–10). Merged rows keep the per-area wording of the evidence; where two areas state different risks, the higher is taken and the disagreement is noted in the Note column.

**Reading the risk values.** `none` — no behaviour, contract or test can notice the removal. `low` — a mechanical edit; a grep or the named test tells you whether you are safe. `medium` — behaviour-visible, or needs coordinated edits across files and tests. `high` — touches a stored format, a contract or a reachability question; do not remove without an owner decision.
