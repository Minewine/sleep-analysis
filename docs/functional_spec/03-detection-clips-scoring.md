# 3. Detection, clips and scoring

**Scope:** `monitoring/detection`, `monitoring/features`, ~~`monitoring/eval`~~, ~~`monitoring/replay`~~ (both moved to `app/src/test/java/com/sleepanalysis/monitoring/{eval,replay}/` in consolidation T2 — main has no eval/replay sources), `data/files/ClipStore.kt`, `domain/scoring`, and the level and clip policies of `monitoring/audio` (ClipPolicy, SpectralGatePolicy, NoiseFloorCache, AmbientLevelBuckets) with their unit tests. AudioEngine is read only at its call sites into these files; capture loop, ring buffer, WAV I/O and event persistence are section 2.
**Requirement prefix:** DET — rows in 3.3 are numbered `DET-01`, `DET-02`, …
**Status values:** `tested` = a unit test asserts it (cited; body opened) · `device-verified` = evidence from a real night or device run exists (cited) · `unverified` = implemented, no asserting test was opened · `not implemented` = designed or documented only.

## 3.1 What the user gets

1. Loud, low-band, tonal and periodic windows are flagged as snoring hop by hop; other loud windows are flagged as loud acoustic events [DET-01..DET-11].
2. Flags become discrete burst-level events: 2 active hops open, 4 quiet hops close, at least 2 s long, and a silence of 2 s or more splits [DET-14..DET-17].
3. Each event carries its type, peak and mean level, intensity tier, confidence and the detector's decision inputs, which the report's event inspector can show [DET-22..DET-25].
4. Events under the room's ambient level plus margin, mic-self-noise rumble, and breathing trains in ultra-quiet rooms are stored as other noise, never as snore [DET-18, DET-19].
5. Up to 8 s of WAV per event is saved, spread over the night by an hourly and a nightly budget, with the loudest moments guaranteed a clip through a loudness reserve and a per-type bout cooldown [DET-26..DET-33].
6. Clip files are gain-controlled and peak-limited to −1 dBFS; orphaned files are swept [DET-34, DET-35].
7. The night gets a 0–100 snore score from snoring share and intensity; a user label overrides the detector in every snoring aggregate [DET-36..DET-40].
8. The detector version and the score formula version are stored with the data, and comparisons across different versions are flagged [DET-41..DET-43].
9. Quiet, idle windows skip the spectral computation without changing any stored result; ambient level buckets tell quiet sleep from no capture and from a silenced microphone [DET-12, DET-13].
10. Developers can replay labeled clips offline and get per-label precision and recall [DET-44..DET-47].

### 3.2.2 Flows

#### Flow: one analyzed hop (live, capture thread)

Steps 1, 4 and 8 belong to AudioEngine (section 2); they are listed because they feed this area.

1. AudioEngine.analyzeWindow (`monitoring/audio/AudioEngine.kt` · `analyzeWindow`) — window dBFS = 20·log10(rms/32768) over the 1 s window of micGain-scaled samples (−100.0 for digital silence); low-band ratio = rms(one-pole low-passed window)/rms(window), clamped to 0..1.
2. NoiseFloorCache.onWindowPushed (`monitoring/audio/NoiseFloorCache.kt` · `onWindowPushed`) — the window level enters the 240-slot ring and the 20th-percentile floor is refreshed; it is always read after the push, so it includes the current window.
3. AmbientLevelBuckets.add — the window level is folded into its 5-minute epoch bucket.
4. Thermal throttle: a throttled odd hop returns here, counted as skipped and never classified (section 2).
5. SpectralGatePolicy.shouldComputeSpectral(dbfs, floor, detectorBusy = not EventDetector.isIdle) — SpectralFeatures.Workspace.compute runs only when it returns true; otherwise the spectral fields stay null.
6. AudioFeatures → SnoreDetector.classify → DetectionResult(snore, loudAcousticEvent, confidence).
7. EventDetector.update(timestampMs = end stamp of the window in engine-relative ms) → zero or more finalized EventDrafts.
8. AudioEngine.deliver(drafts) → ClipPolicy.decide → optional ClipStore.writeClip → Listener.onEventFinalized (section 2 persists the event).

#### Flow: classifier — EnergySnoreDetector.classify, gates in order

1. loud — windowDbfs ≥ noiseFloorDbfs + LOUD_DELTA_DB (12 dB). Sets loudAcousticEvent whatever the other gates say.
2. energy — loud AND snr ≥ SNORE_MIN_SNR_DB (8 dB) AND lowBandRatio ≥ LOW_BAND_RATIO_MIN (0.55), with snr = windowDbfs − noiseFloorDbfs.
3. spectral (only when spectralFlatness is non-null and the gate is enabled) — flatness ≤ SPECTRAL_FLATNESS_MAX (0.55) AND (harmonicRatio ≥ HARMONICITY_MIN (0.30) or harmonicRatio is null).
4. rhythm (only when the spectral gate is active and enabled) — snr ≥ STRONG_SNR_DB (20 dB) OR at least one snore window accepted within RHYTHM_WINDOW_MS (8 000 ms).
5. snore = all of 2 to 4. An accepted snore window's stamp is appended to the rhythm memory; a rejected window never enters it.
6. confidence — 0.0 when not snore, otherwise the formula in DET-05.

Conditional gates of the shipped detector energy-heuristic-v3-peakgate: gate 3 and gate 4 only exist while spectral features are present; the event-level peak tier (DET-19) only arms when the event's mean noise floor is ≤ −80 dBFS; the stricter clip margin (DET-26) only during the first 30 minutes after capture start.

#### Flow: event shaping — EventDetector.update

1. expireDeferred — held finals whose stamp exceeds the bound in DET-20 are returned first.
2. If the window is snore and the OTHER_NOISE machine is active, that machine is closed now (snore precedence).
3. Machine.step on the snore machine with flag = snore. Idle → pending at the first flagged hop; pending → active after ENTER_WINDOWS flagged hops (start backdated one window); active → closed after EXIT_WINDOWS unflagged hops.
4. Machine.step on the other-noise machine with flag = loud AND NOT snore AND snore machine not active.
5. close (`monitoring/detection/EventDetector.kt` · `close`) — the event-level gates (DET-18, DET-19) decide the final type; deferOrFold then holds the draft, folds it, or drops it when it is too short.
6. flush (session end) closes both machines and returns every held and still-open draft by start time.

#### Flow: clip decision and write — AudioEngine.deliver

1. eventStartEpochMs = captureStartEpochMs + draft.startMs; nothing is clipped while captureStartEpochMs is 0.
2. ClipPolicy.decide(meanDbfs, noiseFloorDbfs = the event's mean noise floor, peakDbfs, eventStartEpochMs, captureStartEpochMs, type) → Decision, gates evaluated in the order level, loudness reserve, bout cooldown, nightly cap, hourly cap (DET-26..DET-29).
3. WRITE or WRITE_LOUD_RESERVE → RingBuffer.holdsEventAudio must confirm that the event end is still inside the 8 s ring; otherwise clip_horizon_skipped is logged, nothing is written and nothing is booked.
4. clipRing.snapshotLast (the last 8 s of RAW samples) → ClipStore.writeClip → relative path, duration, file size, appliedGain, filePeakDbfs.
5. ClipPolicy.onWritten runs only after a successful write and books the budget and the cooldown anchor.
6. SKIP_LEVEL only increments levelGateSkipped; SKIP_BUDGET logs clip_budget_skipped and SKIP_COOLDOWN logs clip_cooldown_skipped, each once per hour bucket.
7. Listener.onEventFinalized(draft, clip fields or nulls) — the event is always handed to section 2 for persistence.

#### Flow: scoring at finalize (data layer; section 4 owns the transaction)

1. SessionRepository loads the session's events → SnoreMath.eventsCountingAsSnore.
2. snoreSeconds = SnoreMath.unionDurationMs(spans)/1000; tierSeconds = per intensity tier, each a union.
3. sharePercent = snoreSeconds·100/capturedSeconds when capturedSeconds > 0, otherwise 0.
4. score = SnoreScore.compute(snoreSeconds, capturedSeconds, tierSeconds).
5. Measurement rows as in DET-39; the SNORE_SCORE row stores modelVersion = SnoreScore.FORMULA_VERSION and notes = SnoreScore.breakdownNotes.
6. The report recompute (ReportAssembler) and the label-corrected rewrite (SessionRepository, rows flagged label_corrected=true) call the same functions; report_snapshot.scoreVersion is always FORMULA_VERSION.

#### Flow: offline fixture evaluation (developer tooling)

1. `tools/export_fixtures.py` exports labeled clips and a manifest (the manifest's floor_dbfs is the replay pre-roll floor).
2. The Gradle task evalFixtures in `app/build.gradle.kts` runs FixtureEvalRunTest only; the test is skipped unless fixtures/manifest.json exists (system property sleep.fixtures.dir overrides the directory).
3. FixtureEvaluator.evaluate → per entry: WavReader.readPcm16Mono → detector.reset() → ReplayEngine.replay → ClipScore.
4. One-vs-rest ClassMetrics per label; writeJson → build/eval-report.json; summaryText → stdout.

## 3.6 Contract boundaries — what a refactor must not break

**Persisted formats**
- acoustic_event: type (SNORE or OTHER_NOISE), startMs, endMs, durationMs, offsetMs (TZ-stable canonical UTC offset), peakDbfs, meanDbfs, intensityTier (QUIET / LIGHT / LOUD / EPIC), confidence, modelVersion NOT NULL, features_json, later clipId and userLabel with labeledAt; written by section 2 through `data/repo/SessionRepository.kt` · insertEvent.
- features_json keys, additive only: `enter_windows`, `exit_windows`, `open_windows`, `close_windows`, `snr_peak_db`, `snr_mean_db`, `low_band_ratio_peak`, `low_band_ratio_mean`, `noise_floor_dbfs`, `spectral_flatness_mean`, `harmonic_ratio_mean`. Readers: `ui/report/ReportScreen.kt` (event inspector) and `tools/export_fixtures.py` (noise floor for the replay pre-roll).
- audio_clip: filePath relative to the clip root, durationMs, sizeBytes, sampleRate, appliedGain (2.5 on pre-schema-v11 rows), filePeakDbfs (NULL pre-schema-v11).
- Clip files: clip root / session_<id> / evt_<type-lowercase>_<eventStartEpochMs>_<counter>.wav, PCM16 mono 16 kHz, at most 8 s; swept by `work/RetentionWorker.kt` · deleteOrphans.
- SNORE_SCORE measurement row: value = integer score, modelVersion = score formula version, notes = breakdown string of the form `snore-score-v4: share 43.8/60 + intensity 9.7/40 (tier weight 0.243)`.
- Developer output build/eval-report.json with the keys listed in 3.2.3.

**Version strings stored with data**
- Detector: SessionRepository.MODEL_VERSION = `energy-heuristic-v3-peakgate`, duplicated as FixtureEvaluator.DETECTOR_VERSION. Stamped on every acoustic_event.modelVersion (~~with the suffix `+dbg1` while the debug enter-1 preference is on~~ — the debug toggle was removed in consolidation T3; the suffix is no longer written, stored `+dbg1` rows stay readable), report_snapshot.detectorVersion, the model registry of kind DETECTOR, the session log event `detector_config`, the eval report key `detector_version`, and the modelVersion column of `tools/export_night_csv.py`.
- Score: SnoreScore.FORMULA_VERSION = `snore-score-v4`. Stamped on the modelVersion of SNORE_SCORE measurement rows (finalize and label-corrected rewrite), as the prefix of that row's notes, on report_snapshot.scoreVersion and in the model registry of kind SCORE.
- Stored results that depend on the detector version: the event rows themselves and everything derived from them — SNORE_TIME, SNORE_SHARE_OF_NIGHT, SNORE_SCORE, validity, quiet gaps, bouts, trends, insights. Stored results that depend on the score version: the SNORE_SCORE row and the snapshot's score.
- Consumers that gate on the versions: `data/db/dao/Daos.kt` · firstSnoreModelVersion feeds sameDetectorVersion of the previous-night summary, sameScoreFormula compares the stored SNORE_SCORE modelVersion with FORMULA_VERSION, `domain/report/TrendAssembler.kt` · formulaChanged raises the trend flags, `domain/insights/PersonalBaseline.kt` filters on both versions, `data/repo/SessionRepository.kt` · currentModelVersions resolves the current pair.
- Rows written by earlier versions are read as they are and never re-detected; `ui/trends/TrendsScreen.kt` strips the prefix energy-heuristic- for display.

**Platform contracts**
- None in this area: no manifest entry, permission, intent or notification. The microphone service that feeds it is section 2.

**Cross-area interfaces**
- Into this area: AudioEngine (section 2) constructs EventDetector, ClipPolicy, NoiseFloorCache, AmbientLevelBuckets and a SpectralFeatures.Workspace from `monitoring/audio/AudioEngine.kt` · Config and drives them from `monitoring/audio/AudioEngine.kt` · analyzeWindow; the detector is injected through `di/AppModule.kt` · provideSnoreDetector.
- Out of this area: Listener.onEventFinalized(draft, clipRelativePath, clipDurationMs, clipSizeBytes, appliedGain, filePeakDbfs) is answered by `monitoring/service/MonitorService.kt` · insertEvent; the counters levelGateSkipped, spectralSkipped and clipBytes and the ambient bucket snapshot go into the session statistics of section 2.
- Consumers of the scoring functions: SessionRepository (finalize, label rewrite, snapshot), `domain/report/ReportAssembler.kt` · compute for the live recompute, `data/repo/ValidityCalculator.kt`, `domain/report/TrendAssembler.kt` and `domain/insights/InsightRules.kt`, all through SnoreMath.eventsCountingAsSnore and SnoreMath.unionDurationMs; `domain/report/Bouts.kt` uses only SnoreMath.unionDurationMs and filters on the detected type itself, ignoring user labels — bouts are label-unaware.
- Storage: `work/RetentionWorker.kt` · deleteOrphans; session deletion through `data/repo/SessionRepository.kt` · deleteSessionClips and deleteAll; playback, labeling and validity checks resolve files with `data/files/ClipStore.kt` · absolutePath.
